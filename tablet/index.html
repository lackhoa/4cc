One page per idea. The name of a document is its directory under tablet/pages/ and its URL.

Construction

  • loomis-head — the Loomis ball-and-plane head built step by step, a slider per ratio, the book's numbers as presets.
  • skull-ball — where the ball sits in a real skull: three fits (Frankfurt sphere, cranium sphere, cranium ellipsoid) with heat maps and numbers.
  • skull-side-cuts — Loomis's side slices measured on the skull: the cranium sphere clipped by two mirrored planes, one half_width fitted to the flats, before/after heat maps.
  • skull-brow-line — Loomis's brow line (the ball's equator) on that ball: how far the glabella, the eye socket and the ear hole sit from it, in mm and radii.
  • skull-face-thirds — Loomis's face units on the skull and mandible: brow to nose base and nose base to chin, against Plate 1 (0.71 r) and Plate 18 (2/3 r).
  • skull-mouth-jaw — the mouth line (the upper teeth's incisor tip) as a fraction of nose base to chin, and the jaw angle's height against it, vs Finch's "halfway, a little higher" and "level with the mouth".
  • skull-cheekbones — the zygion (the arch's widest point) against the nose line and against Loomis's side plane: the first width rule, with a horizontal section at the cheekbone's height.
  • skull-eyes — the eye sockets' corners and centers: "five eyes wide" tested on the eye centers (2/5 of the cheekbone width apart), the eye line against the brow and nose lines; the socket's width shown without a verdict.
