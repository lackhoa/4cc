The cheekbones

The first width rule. David Finch puts the cheekbones "just above" the nose line, at the outside of the face; Loomis cuts the ball's sides at one side plane and hangs the face from it. On bone that is two points on the zygomatic arch: the zygion, its widest point, answers the width question (inside, on, or outside the side plane); the zygomatic bottom, the lowest point of its underside, is the edge Finch draws, and answers how far above the nose line the cheekbone line sits. What the skull says

  • Which plane is the side plane. Loomis's side cut is fitted to the vault (the widest point of the braincase, the temple region). If the zygion is wider than that, the cheekbone stands proud of the side plane and the face is wider than the ball's flat, which is what the three-quarter view has to show.
  • The zygion is off the midline. It is the widest point of the arch, so the useful section is horizontal, not the midline cut: the cut at its height shows the arch, the braincase and the ball's circle at that height together.
  • Two points, two rules. Finch's "just above the nose line" is the arch's lower edge, the line a pencil follows under the cheek; the zygion is a few mm higher and is only the width. Quoting the zygion's height against Finch overstates the gap.
  • One skull. Cheekbone width is one of the most variable head proportions; this is one data point (male, see the models' README).

Glossary

brow line
The ball's horizontal section at the height of its center; Loomis's eyebrow line. Measured on the skull on the skull-brow-line page.
zygion
The most lateral point of the zygomatic arch, the cheekbone's widest point. Picked by hand on this page on one side, mirrored to the other, stored in the skull's landmarks file as zygion.
zygomatic bottom
The lowest point of the zygomatic arch: its front end, where the arch turns into the cheek body under the eye socket's outer corner (Khoa's pick; the underside further back, under the zygion, runs 7-9 mm higher). Picked by hand on this page, mirrored, stored as zygomatic_bottom.
nose line
The nasal spine's height, as on skull-face-thirds, from the landmarks file.
side plane
Loomis's flat on the side of the ball: the mirrored planes at the vault's widest point, from skull-side-cuts (fit_side_planes_mirrored).
