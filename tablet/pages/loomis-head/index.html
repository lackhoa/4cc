The Loomis head, step by step

Andrew Loomis, Drawing the Head and Hands (1956), Plate 1 and Plate 18, drawn in 3D. Every length is in ball radii. main menu

Steps

  1. The ball. The cranium is a ball. Everything else is measured against its radius, so the ball is the one thing to draw at the right size.
  2. Slice the sides. A head is not as wide as it is tall: cut a fairly thin slice off each side of the ball. The flat disc left behind is the side plane. Its vertical center line is where the ear sits; the brow line crosses it horizontally.
  3. Brow line and center line. The brow line is the ball's equator; tilting it tilts the whole head. The center line runs over the front of the ball from the top pole down to where the face will hang, then straight down to the chin. It swings sideways as the head turns.
  4. Hairline, nose, chin. Loomis divides the face into three equal units: brow to hairline, brow to nose base, nose base to chin. The hairline is halfway from the brow up to the top of the ball. Nose and chin lines hang below the ball, on the face.
  5. Jaw. From the ear (the center of the side plane) the jaw line drops to the jaw corner and turns forward to the chin. Its width is not a number in the book, hence the slider.
  6. Ear. On the side plane, a little behind its center line; one unit tall, from the brow line down to the nose line.

Where the numbers come from

  • Plate 1: hairline halfway from the brow line to the top of the ball (+0.5 r); nose base and chin "about equal to the forehead", so −0.5 r and −1.0 r.
  • Plate 18: the whole head is 3.5 units tall and brow to nose is one unit. With the ball top at the top of the head, the ball radius is 1.5 units, so one unit is 2/3 r (nose −0.667 r, chin −1.333 r).
  • Sliders cover what the book says in words only: "a fairly thin slice", jaw width, ear placement, how far forward the chin sits.

Glossary

ball
The sphere standing in for the cranium. Radius r = 1 on this page; ball center is the origin.
brow line
The ball's equator. The eyebrows sit on it; the ear's top is level with it.
side plane
The flat disc left when a slice is cut off the side of the ball. Its center is where the ear attaches and where the jaw line starts.
unit
One third of the face: brow to hairline = brow to nose base = nose base to chin. Plate 1 uses 0.5 r, Plate 18 uses 2/3 r.
center line
The head's symmetry line, seen on the front of the ball and down the face. Together with the brow line it fixes the head's turn and tilt.
