Fit the head to Loomis's school girl

The template head (document skull-zanatomy: skull and skin drawing) warped so that its landmarks land on their marks on Loomis's plate. This page is where the fit is tuned: drag a mark and the fitted head follows. The fitted head is computed here from the template and the landmarks, every time either changes; it is saved nowhere. The landmarks are saved on every edit. Draw the hairline on the fitted head.

1. The fitted head on the plate

A cross is a mark read from this view, a circle a guessed one; the line runs from the mark to where the fitted landmark is. Drag a mark to move it, drag the dashed midline to move the front view's centre. The orange lines are the hairline: the skin strokes drawn on the surface or ending at a point pinned to it. Double-tap an empty spot to refit the view.

2. Landmarks

"Add landmark", then tap a point on the head model below: the new landmark's marks start where that point is, so it pulls nothing until you drag them. Tap a landmark (here or on the plate) to select it.

3. The fitted head, turned

Drag to turn. The second half is the mirror of the drawn one.

4. The template before the fit

The template as drawn, un-warped, on the same plate: the lines show how far each landmark has to move.

5. How far each landmark is from its mark

In plate pixels (the head is about 610 pixels tall). "Read from" says which views the mark was read off; the rest of a mark's position is a guess, because hair or the face hides the point there.

6. What is off (as of 2026-10-04)

  • Landmarks that miss. A landmark on a vertex that is pinned to a stroke rides that stroke, so it cannot be put on its cross exactly. These misses run along the curve; the curves themselves sit on the drawn lines.
  • Nose bridge. The fitted bridge is straight, the drawn one is slightly scooped. No landmark sits there.
  • Lips. The plate's front and profile disagree by about 10 pixels on lip heights. The profile is used.
  • Under the hair. Top of head, back of head and the widest point of the cranium are guesses.
  • The skull. Only the skin can be checked against the plate. The fitted skull is a guess carried by the warp.
