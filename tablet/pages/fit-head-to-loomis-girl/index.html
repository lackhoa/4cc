Fitting the head to Loomis's school girl

The template is the head we drew on the Z-Anatomy model: document skull-zanatomy, skull and skin. The target is Loomis's plate of a school girl, a front view and a profile. The fit moves the template's curves until they match the plate, and writes the result to document loomis-school-girl. This page only shows the fit: nothing here can be edited. main menu

1. The template on the plate, before the fit

The template drawn over the plate at the plate's scale, chin bottom on chin bottom. Each green cross is a landmark marked on the plate. Each green dot is the template vertex that belongs to it, and the green line between them is the move the fit has to make.

plate image missing: copy the scan into tablet/public/

These three controls act on all four plate views of this page. In a view: drag to move, wheel or pinch to zoom, double tap to fit the head again. red = skin, blue = skull.

2. The fitted head on the plate

One smooth warp of space carries every template landmark to its cross, and every curve of the drawing goes through the same warp. A dot that is not on its cross is a landmark the fit missed; the green line shows by how much.

3. The fitted head, turned

The same document seen from any side, with its surfaces. The surfaces hide the lines behind them; untick them and the far side of the head shows through the near side.

drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

5. What is off (as of 2026-10-04)

  • Landmarks that miss. A landmark on a vertex that is pinned to a stroke rides that stroke, so it cannot be put on its cross exactly. These misses run along the curve; the curves themselves sit on the drawn lines.
  • Nose bridge. The fitted bridge is straight, the drawn one is slightly scooped. No landmark sits there.
  • Lips. The plate's front and profile disagree by about 10 pixels on lip heights. The profile is used.
  • Under the hair. Top of head, back of head and the widest point of the cranium are guesses.
  • The skull. Only the skin can be checked against the plate. The fitted skull is a guess carried by the warp.
