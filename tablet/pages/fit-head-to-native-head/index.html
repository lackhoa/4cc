Fit the head to the native head

The scan head (document skull-zanatomy, drawn mirrored, its centre plane closed) warped so that its landmarks land on their targets on the native head (document native-head). Drag a mark and the fitted head follows. The fit here is live and saved nowhere; the landmarks are saved to fit-landmarks/native-head.json on every edit. The document head-corrected-trial (what compare-two-heads shows) changes only when npx tsx tmp/make_corrected_head_document.ts head-corrected-trial is run again.

1. The fitted head on the native head

Blue: the native head's lines; red: the fitted head. A cross is a landmark's target; the line runs from it to where the fitted landmark is. A target on a native vertex follows that vertex; dragging a mark turns its target into a fixed position, starting from where the vertex is. In front a drag moves the target sideways and up or down (only up or down on the midline), in profile forward or back and up or down. Double-tap an empty spot to refit the view.

2. Landmarks

"Add landmark", then tap a point on the head model below: the new landmark's target starts where the fitted head has that point, so it pulls nothing until you drag it. Tap a landmark (here or in the views) to select it. "Reset" goes back to the landmarks as this page loaded them.

3. The fitted head, turned

Drag to turn. The second half is the mirror of the drawn one.

4. The scan head before the fit

The scan head un-warped, on the native head: the lines show how far each landmark has to move.
