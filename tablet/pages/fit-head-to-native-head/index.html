Fit the head to the native head

Move the crosses until the red head sits right on the blue head.

"Add landmark", then tap a red line: the new landmark starts where the red head has that point. Let a dragged cross go next to a blue vertex (a ring shows it) to pin it there; hold Alt while dragging to not pin. Ctrl + wheel or a pinch zooms, a drag on empty space moves the picture, a double tap on it fits the head again.
