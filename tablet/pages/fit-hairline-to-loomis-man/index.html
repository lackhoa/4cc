Fitting the hairline to Loomis's planes head

The template head skull-zanatomy over Loomis's planes-head plates, a front view and a profile. The head is not changed: the plates are scaled and moved to it by a few marks each, and the hairline is drawn on the plates and lifted onto the skin as on-surface strokes. main menu

1. Register the plates

Drag the green marks onto the plate. Each mark is a point of the head (to start: brow, nasion and nose base in both views, the ear hole in profile). Each view gets one scale and one offset, least squares over its marks (no rotation, no deformation); the head's midline is the white line in front. The head's own points are the white dots: the gap from a white dot to its green mark is that mark's residual, so if the plate and the head disagree the marks are spread over the disagreement rather than any one of them landing exactly.

More marks: "add landmark", then tap a point of the head model (the white dots are the stroke ends; anywhere along a stroke works too). The new mark starts where the registration puts that point in each view; drag it to where the plate has it. A tap on a mark, in a plate view or on the head model, selects it for renaming or removing.

In a plate view: drag to move (in "move" mode; the other modes drag a mark or draw; the middle mouse button moves in every mode), wheel or pinch to zoom, double tap to fit the plate again. On the head model: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Undo (or Ctrl+Z) takes back mark drags, adds, removes, renames, hairline curves, handle drags and clears; not a lift.

2. Draw and lift the hairline

In "draw hairline" mode the hairline is a bunch of bezier curves per view: a drag from empty space places a straight curve; then its four points are handles: drag an end to move it (let it go on another curve's end to join them), drag a hollow handle to bend the curve. Undo takes back the last one. The arch in front, the forehead-to-ear run in profile. "Lift" casts both drawings onto the skin, drops the grazing hits, merges them by angle around the head, and writes the chain into skull-zanatomy as on-surface strokes, replacing the strokes of the last lift. Teal dots are the merged hits of the last lift.

Marks and hairlines are saved to tablet/fit-landmarks/loomis-planes-man.json as you change them.
