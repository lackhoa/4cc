Fitting the hairline to Loomis's planes head

The template head skull-zanatomy fitted to Loomis's planes-head plates, a front view and a profile, with the sketchpad below to draw the hairline on the template as on-surface skin strokes. The plate views show where those strokes land on the plates. main menu

1. The plates

The green marks are the landmarks of the fit: each is a point of the template and where it must land on the plate (to start: brow, nasion, nose base and ear hole, then the landmarks of the Loomis girl plate, placed where the first four put them). The plate views show the template warped so that every landmark lands on its marks (a thin-plate fit, stiffness 0, the same as fit-head-to-loomis-girl). The white dots are where the template's own points fall before the fit: the gap to the green mark is what the fit closes. Drag the marks onto the plate's features; the fit follows.

In a plate view: drag to move (in "move" mode; "drag marks" drags a mark; the middle mouse button moves in every mode), wheel or pinch to zoom, double tap to fit the plate again. A tap on a mark selects it for renaming or removing. Undo (or Ctrl+Z, when the plates were touched last) takes back mark drags, removes, renames and resets. Marks are saved to tablet/fit-landmarks/loomis-planes-man.json as you change them; the stiffness is edited in that file.

2. Draw the hairline

The sketchpad of skin-draw, on the same document: draw the hairline with the line tool in "on surface" mode on the skin layer. Every on-surface skin stroke is highlighted in orange over the plates above, through the fit, as you draw. Ctrl+Z undoes here when the sketchpad was touched last.
