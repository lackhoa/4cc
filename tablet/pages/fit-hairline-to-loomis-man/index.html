Fitting the hairline to Loomis's planes head

The template head skull-zanatomy over Loomis's planes-head plates, a front view and a profile. The head is not changed: the plates are scaled and moved to it by a few marks each, and the hairline is drawn on the plates and lifted onto the skin as on-surface strokes. main menu

1. Register the plates

Drag the green marks onto the plate: brow, nasion and nose base in both views, the midline in front, the ear hole in profile. Each view gets one scale and one offset, least squares over its marks (no rotation, no deformation). The head's own brow, nasion, nose base and ear hole are the white dots: the gap from a white dot to its green mark is that mark's residual, so if the plate and the head disagree the marks are spread over the disagreement rather than any one of them landing exactly.

In a view: drag to move (in "move" mode; the other modes drag a mark or draw), wheel or pinch to zoom, double tap to fit the plate again.

2. Draw and lift the hairline

In "draw hairline" mode, one drag per view draws the hairline (a new drag replaces the old one): the arch in front, the forehead-to-ear run in profile. "Lift" casts both drawings onto the skin, drops the grazing hits, merges them by angle around the head, and writes the chain into skull-zanatomy as on-surface strokes, replacing the strokes of the last lift. Teal dots are the merged hits of the last lift.

Marks and hairlines are saved to tablet/fit-landmarks/loomis-planes-man.json as you change them.
