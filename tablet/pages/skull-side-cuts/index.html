Slicing the sides off the ball

Loomis cuts "a fairly thin slice" off each side of the ball (see loomis-head, step 2). This page does it his way on the Z-Anatomy skull: a ball as long as the skull, touching the forehead and the back, then the sides sliced where the skull is widest. The least-squares sphere of skull-ball is kept for comparison. All lengths in mm, in the Frankfurt frame. main menu

The rule, in two sections

The length ball passes through the front-most and the back-most vault vertex near the midline and is centered between them: two points fix it, nothing is averaged. half_width is the distance from the midline to the widest vault vertex, the same on both sides. Left: the skull cut along the midline, seen from the right, where "touches the forehead and the back" is visible and so is the price, the ball standing above the skull top. Right: the skull cut across, through the ball's center, seen from the front, where a slice is a straight line.

What the cuts buy

  • a. least-squares sphere. The cranium sphere from skull-ball (outer surface only now). It is a compromise sitting in the middle of the egg: the forehead and the back stick out (red), and it is barely wider than the skull, so there is nothing to slice off its sides. The book's cut cannot be measured from this ball.
  • b. length ball. The ball as Loomis states it: as long as the skull. It touches the forehead and the back by construction, and now the whole skull sits inside it (blue everywhere): the sides by a lot, the top by some. The cranium is longer than it is tall, so a ball this long stands above the skull top; that number is the "above the skull top" column, and it is the next thing a construction would fix (a flatter top).
  • c. length ball with side cuts. The same ball sliced where the skull is widest. The blue on the sides goes pale; front and back are unchanged. The radii number is the one to compare with Loomis's 0.8.

Glossary

vault
The skull vertices the fits see: above the plane through the two porions and the glabella (the cranium cut, same as skull-ball's candidates b and c), outer surface only.
length ball
The sphere whose diameter is the skull's length: through the front-most and back-most vault vertices within 10 mm of the midline, centered between them.
half_width
Distance from the midline to each cut plane, mm: the widest vault vertex. In Loomis's terms the slice depth is radius − half_width.
rim
The circle where a cut plane meets the ball: the edge of Loomis's side plane, whose vertical center line carries the ear.
