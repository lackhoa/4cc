1. The fitted head on the plate

Where the fitted head comes from: it is the template skull-zanatomy, the document you draw on below, warped so that the landmarks of the page fit-head-to-loomis-girl land on their marks on this plate. It is computed here, after every edit below, and saved nowhere. The landmarks are read once, when this page opens: to change the fit, move the marks there, then reload this page.

The orange lines are the hairline: the skin strokes drawn on the surface, or ending at a point pinned to it. The landmarks' marks are not drawn here, so they do not cover it. Double-tap a view to fit the plate again.

2. Draw the hairline

The sketchpad of skin-draw, on the same document. Draw a line on the skin layer, select it and press "on surface", and pin its ends to the surface. The plate views above follow as you draw. Ctrl+Z undoes here when the sketchpad was touched last.
