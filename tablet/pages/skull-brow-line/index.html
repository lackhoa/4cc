The brow line

Loomis's next step after the side cuts (see loomis-head, step 3): the horizontal center line of the side plane, carried around the front of the ball. It is the ball's equator, and the eyebrows are supposed to sit on it. This page draws that line on the ball of skull-side-cuts (the length ball with side cuts) and measures how far the real brow, the eye socket and the ear hole are from it. No fit, no new shape. The line on the skull

drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. yellow ring = the brow line, at the height of the ball's center; on the side planes it is their horizontal center line, at the front and back it is the ball's equator. blue = the cut planes and their rims. Markers: glabella, orbit rim, orbitale, the two porions. In the section the same landmarks are dots with their height above (+) or below (−) the line.

Does his line land on the brow?

  • Glabella and orbit rim. Loomis puts the eyebrows on the line. On this skull the line runs through the brow: the glabella a few mm above it, the top of the eye socket a couple of mm below. Close enough to keep as he drew it.
  • Orbitale. The bottom of the eye socket is on the Frankfurt plane by definition (up 0), so its distance below the line is the ball center's height. The eye sits in the socket's upper half; the book does not give an eye line, the ear does that job.
  • Porion. The ear hole is also on the Frankfurt plane. Loomis hangs the ear from the brow line, one unit tall (0.5 r on Plate 1); the hole is inside the ear, so it should come out well below the line and above −0.5 r.

Glossary

brow line
Loomis: the horizontal center line of the side plane, carried around the ball. Here: the ball's horizontal section at the height of its center, a circle clipped by the two cut planes.
glabella
The brow point, the most forward point of the forehead between the eyebrows; picked by hand on the skull-ball page and stored in the landmarks file.
orbit rim
The top edge of the right eye socket, where the eyebrow sits: found on the mesh as the last brow band before the surface falls into the socket (same rule as the eyebrow ruler on skull-ball).
orbitale
The lowest point of the right eye socket's rim, one of the three landmarks that define the Frankfurt plane.
porion
The top of the ear hole, one on each side; the Frankfurt frame's origin is halfway between them.
